Summary

15 years of experience designing digital services for complex domains – from telecom and defense to micromobility and education. I work systematically from insight to finished solution, with a documented ability to deliver results that work for both users and business.

I start by understanding the problem through user interviews and mapping existing solutions. Then I create prototypes that are tested early, so we iterate based on actual user feedback – not assumptions.

My recent assignments include work at the Norwegian Defence Materiel Agency, Whee! and Inspera, where I've worked with interaction design, content design and technical documentation.
