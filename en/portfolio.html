Norwegian Defence Materiel Agency (FMA)

NORCCIS Help Documentation

Translation, interaction design, accessibility, and user testing of NORCCIS help documentation.

The Problem: NORCCIS help documentation was written by different people over time, without consistent structure or language. Users – many with English as their first language – struggled to find relevant information.

Process: Started by mapping existing documentation and identifying the biggest pain points through conversations with users from different management areas. Developed proto-personas to understand the needs of experienced vs. new users. Mapped out information architecture in Figjam and tested early sketches on navigation and search functionality.

Methodology: Evaluated third-party solutions against technical requirements, security requirements, and usability. Developed standards for accessibility and plain language as a template for English translation.

Result: Delivered wireframes, guidelines for language and visual profile, and validated navigation structure. The standards are now used as a foundation for further content work.

Interaction Design Content Design Accessibility Translation Plain Language User Testing Paligo Documentation

Key Deliverables

  • Wireframes for section pages, search page, and front page
  • Evaluation and selection of third-party solution
  • Standards for accessibility and plain language
  • Visual guidelines
  • User stories
  • User testing

Telia Norway

Springstar

UX Lead for the project merging Get and Telia

The Problem: Telia acquired Get and needed to integrate TV and broadband services into their portfolio. Hundreds of thousands of customers had to be migrated without friction – while two different design systems, customer journeys, and login solutions were being consolidated.

My Role: UX Lead with overall responsibility for user experience on logged-in and logged-out surfaces. Coordinated design resources from both organizations.

Process: Started by mapping existing customer journeys in both companies to identify overlaps and conflicts. Set up prioritization plan together with content lead and tech lead. Conducted design sync every other week to ensure consistency in the design system during the merger.

Methodology: User testing before, during, and after launch – first without company name (confidential phase), then with actual customers from both companies. Designed solutions for complex login/role management where customers could have subscriptions with both.

Result: Launch with minimal customer friction. For customers of both companies, services were improved, and dual login was not a problem.

  • Extensive user insights from both Get and Telia
  • Flow mapping for login and switching between pages during transition
